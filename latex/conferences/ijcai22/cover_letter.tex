	\subsection*{Publication history}
	This article is an extension of previous work presented at a 2019 workshop  (\cite{claes2019user} @HolyGrail@CP, a workshop of the Constraint Programming conference), a 2019 demo (\cite{claes2019zebratutor}@BNAIC, the local Dutch-Belgian AI conference) and an ECAI 2020 conference paper (\cite{bogaerts2020step}).
	
	\subsection*{Elegibility}
	In the ECAI 2020 paper \emph{``Step-wise explanations of constraints satisfaction problems''} (\cite{bogaerts2020step}, we formalized the problem of explaining in a sequence of simple steps, a.k.a. the \emph{explanation sequence}, how to solve constraint satisfaction problems (CSPs).
	
	In this manuscript, we extended the conference paper considerably, namely
	\begin{itemize}
		\item Based on the formalism introduced in (\cite{bogaerts2020step}), we develop nested explanations to break the most difficult steps up in easier steps using \emph{reasoning by contradiction};
		\item Using reasoning by contradiction, we bridge the gap with other existing techniques for explaining \emph{Unsatisfiability}.
		\item We demonstrate the quality and feasibility of our approach, with more detailed examples, and additional experiments with a use case of logic grid puzzles.
	\end{itemize}
	
	\subsection*{Summary}
	Our journal paper bridges the gap between explaining satisfiable and unsatisfiable instances. The work has much matured since the initial ECAI2020 and we hope to reach a broader audience of AI researchers by presenting it in the Journal track of IJCAI2022.
	
	Moreover, since there is a general increasing interest in explainable AI, and since our examples come from puzzle domains, we believe we can deliver a presentation that is accessible to a general AI audience. Therefore, we hope you will accept our paper for the Journal Track.
