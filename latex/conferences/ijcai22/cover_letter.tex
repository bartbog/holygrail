	We would like to submit our manuscript entitled ``A framework for step-wise explaining how to solve constraint satisfaction problems'' by Bart Bogaerts, Emilio Gamba and Tias Guns for publication in the Journal Track of IJCAI-ECAI 2022
	% EMILIO: I'm not sure I should be writing this in full or not, seems like a bit of overkill : ({the 31st International Joint Conference on Artificial Intelligence and the 23rd European Conference on Artificial Intelligence).
	
	This article is an extension of previous papers presented at workshops and conferences \cite{claes2019zebratutor}, \cite{claes2019user}, and \cite{bogaerts2020step}. More importantly, our work builds on ``Step-wise explanations of constraints satisfaction problems'' (\cite{bogaerts2020step}), where we formalize the problem of explaining in a sequence of simple steps, aka the \emph{explanation sequence}, how to solve constraint satisfaction problems (CSPs).
	
	In this manuscript, we extend the formalism introduced in (\cite{bogaerts2020step}), and develop a mechanism using \emph{reasoning by contradiction} to break the most difficult steps up in easier steps, what we call `nested explanation sequences'. By doing so, we provide the user with the ability to \emph{zoom in} on specific parts of the explanation. Contrary to explaining (satisfiable) solutions of CSPs, reasoning by contradiction allows us to bridge the gap with existing techniques for explaining \emph{Unsatisfiability}. Finally, we demonstrate the quality and feasibility of our approach, with more detailed examples, and additional experiments with a use case of logic grid puzzles.
	
	Therefore, we believe that the Journal Track of IJCAI would be an ideal venue as our manuscript tackles explanations in Constraint Solving, a subfield of XAI (eXplainable Artificial Intelligence).
